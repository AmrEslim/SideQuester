How It Works

Simple technology, powerful results

1

GPS Lock

Each device gets its precise location from satellites

2

LoRa Transmission

Coordinates are transmitted via long-range radio

3

Calculate Bearing

Device calculates direction and distance to your friend

4

Follow the Compass

The compass always points toward your friend's location
